# Voice and Audio

- [Whisper](https://github.com/openai/whisper) — speech recognition. Open source.
- [whisper.cpp](https://github.com/ggml-org/whisper.cpp) — efficient local Whisper inference. Open source.
- [Piper](https://github.com/rhasspy/piper) — local text-to-speech. Open source.
- [Kokoro](https://github.com/hexgrad/kokoro) — open-weight text-to-speech model; check model and voice licenses.
